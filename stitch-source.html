Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about switching to solar.

How long do solar panels last?

Our premium solar panels come with a 25-year performance warranty, but they are designed to last 30-40 years with minimal degradation in efficiency over time.

What happens on cloudy days or at night?

Your panels will still generate some power on cloudy days. At night, you will draw from the grid or your battery storage system (if installed). Through net metering, excess power generated during the day can offset nighttime grid usage.

Will solar panels damage my roof?

No. In fact, they often protect the portion of the roof they cover from weather and sun damage. Our installers use specialized mounting hardware that preserves roof integrity and includes a comprehensive leak warranty.

Are there still tax incentives for going solar?

Yes! The federal solar tax credit (ITC) currently allows you to deduct 30% of the cost of installing a solar energy system from your federal taxes. Many states also offer additional local incentives.
